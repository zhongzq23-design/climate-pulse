# Review policy (draft)

## Standard Events

Structured authoritative feeds may be ingested automatically as candidates. Ingestion does not imply publication endorsement or climate attribution.

## Emerging Signals

Cryosphere, ecosystem, coral, biodiversity, species and other less-standardized signals require stronger source triangulation and human scientific review before publication.

## Climate context vs attribution

Local long-term temperature history may be shown as climate background. It must not be presented as proof that anthropogenic warming caused a particular event. Formal attribution evidence is separate and must be explicitly sourced.
