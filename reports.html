How totals are interpreted

Headline population exposure uses only public/significant events with exposure-grade mapped footprints, currently wildfire and tropical-cyclone hazard zones. These footprints are spatially unioned before GHSL population extraction so mapped overlap is counted once.

Flood reported event areas are context-grade, not observed inundation extent, so their population context is reported separately rather than mixed into the cross-hazard headline. Drought polygons are risk-grade and are used for drought/crop context.

Wildfire burned area uses the spatial union of mapped wildfire footprints as the headline, with source-event area sums retained only as a secondary diagnostic. Raw report JSON keeps exact values, while displayed population counts are rounded to the nearest thousand and values below 1,000 are shown as <1,000.
